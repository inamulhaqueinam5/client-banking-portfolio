# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, executive search committees, hiring managers, and corporate leadership evaluating senior banking, foreign trade, branch operations, and regulatory compliance candidates.

## Product Purpose
Deliver an executive-grade personal portfolio website for Zannat Ara Nishat (Senior Banking & Foreign Trade Specialist with 10+ years at National Bank PLC) that transforms static resume credentials into an interactive, high-trust digital presence, facilitating instant qualification review and direct contact.

## Positioning
An experienced banking officer combining 10+ years of frontline branch operations, leadership across major metropolitan branches (Mohakhali, Banani, Gulshan), and specialized expertise in foreign trade / export bill settlement and strict regulatory governance (AML/CFT, Bangladesh Bank guidelines, JAIBB, AIBB).

## Operating Context
Hiring managers and banking executives reviewing candidates in fast-paced corporate recruiting workflows or executive search evaluations, seeking fast proof of leadership, certification authenticity, operational milestones, and contact accessibility across desktop and mobile screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Direct download access to original `resume.pdf`.
- Interactive chronological career timeline detailing branch leadership progression.
- Structured competency matrix highlighting branch operations, trade finance, compliance, and core banking software.
- Governance & credentials showcase featuring JAIBB, AIBB, Cash Risk Mitigation, and AML/CFT certifications.
- Interactive contact inquiry flow with toast feedback and direct `mailto:` triggers.
- Static / App Router client-side performance, responsive across all viewports (Mobile: 375px, Tablet: 768px, Desktop: 1440px).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Zannat Ara Nishat
- Executive identity: Senior Banking & Foreign Trade Specialist / First Executive Officer.
- Tone and voice: Executive, dignified, institutional, authoritative, high-trust, and precise.

## Evidence on Hand
- Candidate resume: `resume.pdf` (local asset in repository).
- Verified career roles at National Bank PLC across Mohakhali, Banani, and Gulshan branches.
- Verified certifications: JAIBB, AIBB (Institute of Bankers, Bangladesh).
- Verified education: M.A. & B.A. in English from Govt. Titumir College.

## Product Principles
1. **Institutional Trust Over Flashiness**: Every visual and structural element must reflect banking-grade rigor, credibility, and elegance.
2. **Instant Scannability**: Executive decision-makers must understand the candidate's core value proposition, scale of experience, and credentials within 5 seconds of landing.
3. **Factual Authenticity**: All metrics, branch assignments, certifications, and operational milestones strictly adhere to verifiable resume data without synthetic embellishments.
4. **Seamless Accessibility**: Flawless responsive performance, legible typography, WCAG AA compliance, and single-click resume download on every device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Targeting WCAG AA compliance with clear visual contrast on dark surfaces, full keyboard navigation support for interactive timeline and filter components, and legible typography hierarchy.
